Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Ellar Aitch (ABN 49 456 554 989) is a one-person app studio in Australia. This policy covers every Ellar Aitch app and this website. I've kept it in plain English. If anything's unclear, email me at hello@ellaraitch.com.

  • No accounts and no tracking in any of my apps. No ads right now, and never in kids apps.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it.
  • If you do share, it's anonymous monthly totals only. I can't tell which device or person they came from.
  • I never sell or share your information.

Saved on your device

Each app saves its progress, scores and settings on your device only. I can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Bloxstalgia

Game counts (optional, off unless you say yes)

After you've finished a few games, Bloxstalgia asks once whether you'd like to share game counts. If you say yes, your device keeps a simple tally for each month: how many games of each mode (Combo Run and Classic) and each theme were played. After the month ends, the next time you're online, it sends that month's totals and then clears them from your device.

The counts don't include your name, scores, dates, times, location or any device ID. You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings → Privacy, which also deletes any totals not yet sent. You can see exactly what's been counted this month in the same place.

Guest play

Guest play sets your scores aside on your device while someone else plays. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Itty Critters Kids

Itty Critters is made for children. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and no third-party ads, analytics or tracking code. It collects no personal information from children.

Level counts (optional, set by a grown-up)

The first time Player profiles is opened, a grown-up decides, behind a parental gate, whether to share level counts. The choice covers every player on the device. If they say yes, the device keeps a monthly total of finished levels for each game (Pour Pals, Little Trails and Sunset Pairs). After the month ends, it sends those three numbers once and clears them from the device.

The totals are for the whole device. They contain no player names, profiles, ages, scores, times or device IDs. A grown-up can turn sharing off at any time in Grown-ups → Player profiles, which also deletes any totals not yet sent.

I've written this with Australia's Privacy Act and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in mind.

Unmuddle

Unmuddle uses your camera to read the colours of your cube. Images are processed on your device only. They're never saved or sent anywhere, and Unmuddle collects nothing.

How shared totals are handled

Shared totals go to a database run for me by Supabase, with servers in Sydney, Australia. When totals arrive, they're added to that month's overall count for each app and the message itself is thrown away. There's no record for each device, only running totals.

My database never stores IP addresses. Like any web service, Supabase's security logs briefly record the network address that each request came from. Those logs delete themselves within 7 days, and I don't use them to identify anyone. Only I see the totals. I never sell or share them.

Feedback emails

Some apps have a Send feedback button. It opens your own email app with a message you can read and change before sending. If you send it, I receive your email address and what you wrote. I use it only to reply and to improve the apps, and I delete it when it's no longer needed.

Ads

None of my apps show ads at the moment. If a future version of an app for everyone shows ads, I'll update this page first, naming the ad provider and what it collects. Kids apps will never show ads.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking, and its fonts are served from the site itself. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as set out in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

  • You can change any sharing choice in the app's settings at any time.
  • Because shared totals are anonymous and combined, there's nothing that links them back to you, so I can't find or delete one person's numbers.
  • To see, correct or delete any email you've sent me, just ask at hello@ellaraitch.com.
  • If you're unhappy with how I've handled a privacy concern, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
