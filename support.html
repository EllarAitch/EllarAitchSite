Common questions

Where are my scores and progress saved?

On your device only. That keeps them private, but it also means deleting the app deletes them.

Can someone else play without messing up my scores? (Bloxstalgia)

Yes. Go to Settings → Progress → Guest play. Your scores are set aside safely, and they come back when guest play ends.

How do I start again from scratch? (Bloxstalgia)

Settings → Progress → Reset progress. It asks you to confirm first.

Flashing lights bother me. Can I turn them down? (Bloxstalgia)

Yes. Go to Settings → Reduce flashing. Celebrations and warnings still show, but as steady glows and single pops, with no full-screen flashes. If your device has Reduce Motion turned on, Bloxstalgia also drops the shakes and particles.

How does the Hardcore world leaderboard work? (Bloxstalgia)

It uses Apple's Game Center, and Bloxstalgia asks before posting anything. Each Hardcore game is checked when it ends, and only games that pass go on the leaderboard and earn badges. The game over screen tells you if a game didn't rank, and why. You can turn posting off in Settings → World leaderboard.

How do I turn sharing on or off?

In Bloxstalgia, go to Settings → Privacy. Itty Critters doesn't share anything, so there's nothing to turn off. See the privacy policy for exactly what's shared.

Flashing lights bother my child. Can I turn them down? (Itty Critters)

Nothing in Itty Critters flashes quickly. To hold twinkles and glows steady too, a grown-up can go to Grown-ups → Player profiles → Reduce flashing.
