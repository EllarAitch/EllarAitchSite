Common questions

Where are my scores and progress saved?

On your device only. That keeps them private, but it also means deleting the app deletes them.

Can someone else play without messing up my scores? (Bloxstalgia)

Yes. Go to Settings → Progress → Guest play. Your scores are set aside safely, and they come back when guest play ends.

How do I start again from scratch? (Bloxstalgia)

Settings → Progress → Reset progress. It asks you to confirm first.

How do I turn sharing on or off?

In Bloxstalgia, go to Settings → Privacy. In Itty Critters, a grown-up can go to Grown-ups → Player profiles. See the privacy policy for exactly what's shared.
